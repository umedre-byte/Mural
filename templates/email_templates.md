# Email Templates for Mural Pitches

Use these templates as starting points. Always personalize with specific details about the business.

---

## Template 1: New Restaurant/Bar Opening

**Subject Lines (pick one):**
- Congratulations on [Restaurant Name] - mural idea
- Art for [Restaurant Name]'s walls?
- Making [Restaurant Name] Instagram-worthy

**Body:**

Hi [Name],

Congratulations on opening [Restaurant Name]! I saw the photos from [source - soft opening/Instagram/article] and love what you're creating in [neighborhood].

I'm a muralist based in NYC with 10+ years of experience creating custom artwork for restaurants. I've worked with [similar client/type], and I think a mural could really complement your [specific detail about their space/concept].

Would you be open to a quick chat about bringing some original art to your walls? Happy to share my portfolio: [link]

Best,
[Your Name]
[Phone] | [Instagram]

## Writing Tips

1. **Keep it short** - Under 150 words for cold outreach
2. **Be specific** - Reference something real about their business
3. **Show don't tell** - Link to portfolio rather than describing it
4. **One clear CTA** - Ask for a chat, not a commitment
5. **Sound human** - Match their vibe (casual for brewery, polished for corporate)
6. **Subject lines** - Keep under 50 characters, make it about them
7. **Timing** - Reach out 2-4 weeks before opening when possible
8. **Follow up** - Once after 5-7 days, then let it go

## Personalization Checklist

Before sending, make sure you've included:

- [ ] Their business name (spelled correctly!)
- [ ] Owner/manager's actual name
- [ ] Specific detail about their business
- [ ] Relevant portfolio link
- [ ] Clear call to action
- [ ] Your contact information
